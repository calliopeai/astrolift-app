## Why this exists

Astrolift is licensed to the public under AGPL-3.0 and to commercial customers
under separate terms. Offering both requires that Calliope Labs Inc. hold rights
in the whole codebase broad enough to license it under terms other than the
AGPL.

Today that is automatic: every commit in this repository was authored by
Calliope Labs Inc. The moment a contribution arrives from someone else under the
AGPL alone, that file can only ever be licensed under the AGPL. Dual licensing
stops working, and recovering the right means finding every past contributor and
asking each to agree, which gets harder every year and is sometimes impossible.

This agreement keeps that door open. It is signed once, before a contributor's
first change is merged.
